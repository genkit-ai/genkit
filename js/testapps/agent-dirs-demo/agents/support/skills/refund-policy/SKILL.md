# Refund policy

ACME's refund policy:

- Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Digital goods are refundable within 14 days if not downloaded.
- To start a refund, verify the order with lookupOrder first, then direct the
  customer to acme.example/refunds with their order id.
