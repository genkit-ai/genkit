# Fieldnote Employee Handbook

Welcome. This is the source of truth for time off, leave, remote work, and
expenses. If a Slack thread and this handbook disagree, the handbook wins.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive **20 PTO days** per calendar year and **10 paid
holidays**. PTO accrues at the start of the year. Unused days roll over up to
5; anything above that is paid out in January.

Part-time employees accrue PTO pro-rata on scheduled hours. Contractors do
not receive PTO.

Request time off in the HR app at least 5 business days ahead for absences
of 3 days or fewer, and 3 weeks ahead for a week or more. Your manager can
waive the notice window for a family emergency.

## Parental leave

Birth, adoptive, and foster parents receive **16 weeks of paid parental
leave**. The 16 weeks may be taken continuously or split across the first
12 months after the child arrives. Health insurance stays on the company
plan for the full leave.

A parent who is not the primary caregiver may take 4 of the 16 weeks
concurrently with the primary caregiver. Tell People Ops before the leave
starts so payroll and coverage are in place.

## Remote work

The default is **3 days in office, 2 days remote** each week. Core hours
are 11:00–15:00 in your office timezone so overlapping meetings stay sane.

Fully remote is available after 6 months if your manager and the role both
support it. A fully remote employee comes to the office for the two
planning weeks each year; Fieldnote books the travel.

## Expenses

Meals while traveling: **$75 per person per meal**, no alcohol on the
company card. Individual expenses over **$200** need manager approval
before you book. Submit receipts within 14 days.

Laptops, monitors, and chairs go through the IT catalog — do not expense
them as a one-off.

## On-call

Engineers on the product rotation cover **one week per quarter**. The
stipend is **$150 per on-call day**, including quiet days. A page that
keeps you up after midnight is a day of PTO you can take the following
week, no questions asked.
